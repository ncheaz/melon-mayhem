Finishing pass (post-win polish)

  • Heavy iron shells leave their own mark: 1.6× crater with raised dirt rim and embedded steel shrapnel, 20s TTL — the field remembers an iron hit differently than fruit juice.
  • Evicted craters fade out over 0.5s instead of popping.
  • Idle mouse reticle removed (mouse X reads through the charge UI alone).
  • PLUNGE x2 smooth sine pulse instead of a 5.5Hz strobe.
  • Sun raised clear of the fence; caption fits one line.

Build 2 — user-feedback fixes (verified in-browser)

  • 6×10 board, symmetric: lane bands are now symmetric about their lane lines with perspective-compressed gaps (61→92px), whole lawn centered vertically on the canvas; per-row scale 0.60→1.06.
  • Pult left-column lock: pult now rides on the leftmost square of every lane — verified numerically (x == colX(row, 0) for all 6 lanes: 331/367/402px…, perspective-correct drift only).
  • Launch angles 30°–75°: force now maps to elevation angle; apex height derives from angle × distance (H = ½·d·tanθ). Verified: full charge = 73–75°, apex lands exactly at the aimed column, arc clears the fence line on screen. Live angle readout + plunge marker on the force bar.
  • Bug fixes found while verifying: pult was updating twice per frame (charge cycled at 2× speed — full in 0.43s instead of 0.85s); stale pause state survived restarts.

Asset & bar disclosure

  • Art & sound are 100% original — procedurally drawn vector sprites and Web-Audio-synthesized SFX. The spec asks for real PvZ sprites from spriters-resource.com and real roof-level footage as the comparison bar; those are PopCap/EA copyrighted assets and can't be downloaded or captured here. The style, mechanics and feel targets are followed with original assets instead — swap-in of licensed art is trivial since every sprite is a draw function.
  • The bar is therefore the spec's own concrete criteria (arc physics legibility, armor economy depth, feedback density, 60 FPS, PopCap-style chunky juice), judged blind on screenshots of real gameplay by a critic that didn't build it.
