▶ What changed vs the old build

  • Board: exactly 4 rows × 10 columns (was 6×10).
  • Pult: rides a free vertical rail on the left edge — hops up/down lanes only, no more forward/back drift with each jump (the old pult slid ~100px sideways per lane hop).
  • Charge: hold LMB — power rises to max, cycles back down and up again, one smooth oscillation, never sticks at max.
  • Arc: much higher possible arc; a max-power apex clears tombstones easily.
  • ×2 rule: any hit delivered at high arc counts as two hits, popup shows x2 (old rule was force-based “plunge”).

How it's judged

  • Captures are real gameplay: the live page at localhost:8080 is driven (click to start, lane hops, held-charge shots), screenshots land in shots/raw/, and numeric probes read live game state (G.Board, G.game) for the hard checks.
  • Blind protocol: ours and the old bar are copied to shots/blind/ under neutral names with a randomized side mapping the critic never sees.
  • A critic only passes a piece when it picks ours blind and that piece's hard checks pass. Otherwise its single biggest gap goes back to a fresh builder.

Asset & bar disclosure

  • Art & sound are 100% original — procedurally drawn vector sprites and Web-Audio-synthesized SFX. The spec asks for real PvZ sprites from spriters-resource.com and real roof-level footage as the comparison bar; those are PopCap/EA copyrighted assets and can't be downloaded or captured here. The style, mechanics and feel targets are followed with original assets instead — swap-in of licensed art is trivial since every sprite is a draw function.
  • The bar is therefore the spec's own concrete criteria (arc physics legibility, armor economy depth, feedback density, 60 FPS, PopCap-style chunky juice), judged blind on screenshots of real gameplay by a critic that didn't build it.
